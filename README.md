**Open to remote work** in machine learning, mobile apps and backend development.

## Projects

| Project | What it does |
|---|---|
| [**InvoiceAI**](https://github.com/SamiHotak/invoice-ai) | Turns invoice and receipt images or PDFs into JSON and Excel, with a confidence level for every value. Reads the total correctly on 95% of 100 test receipts (SROIE test set, my own evaluation). |
| [**CountVision**](https://github.com/SamiHotak/countvision) | Turns a shop's existing cameras into visitor and traffic numbers. Only numbers leave the building: no video and no pictures are stored or sent. |
| [**Product Foundation**](https://github.com/SamiHotak/product-foundation) | A reusable SaaS starter: FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Celery and Next.js, with billing, emails, admin pages and CI. |
| [**Retinal Disease Screening**](https://github.com/SamiHotak/Eye-Disease-Screening-System) | Android app that screens eye (fundus) photos for several diseases offline on the phone and shows attention maps to explain each result. Research project, not a medical device. |
| [**PDDS – Plant Disease Detection**](https://github.com/SamiHotak/Plant-Disease-Detection-System-using-TFLite-Flutter-) | Flutter app that finds 38 plant conditions across 14 crops from a leaf photo, fully offline with TensorFlow Lite. |
| [**Heart Disease Prediction**](https://github.com/SamiHotak/Heart-Disease-Predicrion-System-using-TF-and-TF-Lite-Mobile-Deployment-) | Flutter app with an on-device TensorFlow Lite model that estimates heart disease risk from 13 clinical values. Student project, not a medical device. |
